# Deferred V1 Features

These ideas are intentionally out of scope for Version 1. They are recorded here to preserve extension points without adding dependencies or production code.

## Guest system

- Invitation matrix and private events
- Household permissions, plus-one rules, and personalized schedules

## RSVP

- Meal selections and multi-event attendance
- Transportation choices and reminder/confirmation emails

## Site lifecycle and communication

- Automatic pre-RSVP, RSVP, wedding-week, and post-wedding modes
- Targeted announcement banners and SMS/email updates

## Calendar and photography

- ICS downloads and calendar feeds
- Guest photo upload and post-wedding gallery

## Administration and analytics

- Drag-and-drop page builder, sophisticated audit UI, and multiple editor roles
- Privacy-friendly visitor analytics and RSVP funnel analytics
